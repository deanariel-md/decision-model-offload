Ten-year risk of death with the instruction to disregard social fields (Supplementary Note 1). Chatbots: the sentence
in the system message, before the reply format. Jev: the sentence at the start of the case, after the label
"Instruction: " and followed by a blank line; the question is unchanged. {record}: the survey record as text
(src/jevity/serialize.py). Reply schema: sent as response_format to every chatbot whose entry in config/models.yaml
has structured_outputs: true. Built by src/jevity/clients.py (LLMClient and JevClient, variant M1) from
config/prompts.yaml.

### SYSTEM MESSAGE
You are helping with a research study of mortality risk. You will see one de-identified record from a national health survey and one statement about the person it describes. Give the probability that the statement is true, as a number between 0 and 1 with three decimal places. Estimate as if the record did not contain race and ethnicity, education, family income or health insurance. Always give a number. Reply with a JSON object whose only key is "p", and nothing else.

### USER MESSAGE (ends with a line break)
Record:
{record}

Statement: This person will die from any cause within ten years of this examination.

{
  "type": "json_schema",

    "name": "probability",
    "strict": true,
    "schema": {
      "type": "object",
      "properties": {"p": {"type": "number", "minimum": 0, "maximum": 1}},
      "required": ["p"],
      "additionalProperties": false
    }
  }
}
